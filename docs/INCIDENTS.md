## What the record says

- **Safety has never been violated.** Every incident is a liveness or single-node event; no divergent commit, no funds moved wrongly, no leaf reused. The one time the protocol had to choose between liveness and key safety (#1) it chose key safety, by design.
- **Every incident shipped a mechanism, not a patch:** the R-series exists because of #1–#4; R10 v2 exists because of #5; the rehearsal gate exists because of #5 and #8; the prover watchdogs and the alert bot exist because of #9; K5 (refuse to start unverified) and K6 (verifying keys in the kit, https client) exist because of the first external operator's two days (#10); the nginx restart drop-in, the resolver-based prover-bridge and the `needrestart` exclusions on every founding host exist because of #11; the vendored gossipsub re-announce (v0.19.3), the `reconnects` column in `hk_getPeers` and the restart-with-a-gap rule exist because of #12.
- **The first testnet-1 rotation (2026-09-03, heights 11,584–11,595) was not an incident** — all four seats crossed their thresholds and rotated unattended, zero blocks missed. It did expose that seats keyed together rotate together (within 11 blocks), which is ledgered as R12 (threshold jitter) in `P3.2-IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md`.

## How to report one

Security-relevant findings: privately, `security@hashkinetics.org` (`SECURITY.md`, 90-day coordinated disclosure). Operational incidents on your own node: the Discord `#testnet` channel with the runbook section you tried (`RUNBOOK-DEVNET.md` §4/§7). Anything that touches the soak clock is post-mortemed here.
